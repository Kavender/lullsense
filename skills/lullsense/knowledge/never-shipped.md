# Never shipped (CAM-160 tier 3)

**Status: recorded only. NEVER shipped.** Nothing on this list may enter `claims.yaml`,
`sleep_timing_heuristics.yaml`, `SKILL.md`, `references/`, or a gateway prompt. It exists so a
future edit can see the line was drawn on purpose (CAM-160 spec §5.3; research note
`docs/research/2026-11-07-consult-reasoning-spine.md` §6.3 in lullsense-app). No agent loads
this file; the chat gateway reads only `claims-index.md`, `claims.yaml`, `sources.yaml`.

| Item | Why it stays out |
|---|---|
| Personal thresholds — a specific minute cap ("nap never over N min") or a fixed clock cut-off taken from one consultant's practice or private material | No public source; would read as a rule. Tier 2 uses only public practitioner tables, as ranges with a basis line. |
| Feeding adjustments (amounts, intervals, adding/removing feeds to fix sleep) | Needs weight, growth and pediatric judgment. |
| Night-weaning schedules | Needs normal weight gain and pediatrician confirmation (NHS; AAP via HealthyChildren); the app gives no weaning timetable. |
| Any medicine, melatonin, or iron/supplement dose | Safety boundary: refer to the pediatrician. Iron deficiency → "ask your doctor" only. |
| Pillows, positioners, restraints, weighted or inclined products | Conflicts with AAP 2022 safe sleep. |
| "Over-tired → cortisol → night/early waking" as cause and effect | Correlational only (Scher 2010); counter-evidence (Lassonde 2016). May be named at most as "possibly". |
| "Sleep regression" as a fixed-age event (4 / 8 / 18 months) | Not a diagnostic term; disruption follows skills, not the calendar (Scher & Cohen 2015). |
| Wake windows or "N days of early waking → drop a nap" as hard rules | Practitioner heuristics with no study behind the numbers. |
| Cry-it-out (extinction) as the default method | Offered alongside gradual options; the parent chooses. |
| Any schedule, wake-window, or training advice under 4 months | AASM 2016 gives no range under 4 months; safe sleep and red flags only. |
